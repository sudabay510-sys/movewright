# Movewright — Brand identity

**Slug:** `movewright` · **Tagline:** A week-by-week moving plan, from first box to first night.
**Status:** Early access. Checkout not live; founding list only. No payments processed.

## Positioning
Renters, homeowners, and families moving across town or across the country.

**Promise (honest):** Checklist planning only. We don’t book movers or guarantee costs.

## Personas
- **First big move:** Leaving a first apartment and not sure where to start.
- **Family move:** Kids, pets, and a new school to plan around.
- **Long-distance mover:** New state, new license, new everything.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Decide** — pain: Not sure DIY or movers. → plan: A quote checklist and comparison steps.
2. **Declutter** — pain: Too much stuff, too little time. → plan: Room order starting with the least-used space.
3. **Pack** — pain: The kitchen is still full the night before. → plan: A room-by-room packing sequence.
4. **Arrive** — pain: Can’t find the sheets on night one. → plan: Open-first boxes and a first-week setup.

## Voice & tone
Upbeat project manager with a tape gun: clear, kind, slightly funny.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing (planned terms; not live)
- **8-week move plan** — $19 one-time, 8 weeks, then no auto-renewal unless cancelled
- **12-week move + settle** — $29 one-time, 12 weeks, then no auto-renewal unless cancelled

## Do
- Link only to official sites (USPS, FMCSA, DMV).
- Be clear that terms are fixed and don’t auto-renew.
- Show intro **and** renewal prices before any future charge.
- Label the founding list as “no charge today”.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Sell or share leads with movers.
- Promise cost savings or legal outcomes on leases/deposits.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
